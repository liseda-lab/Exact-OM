# Expanded development profile and parent inventory

`tools.repair.expanded_profile` prepares the first stage of the registered
expanded profile/corpus study. The campaign artifact `profile-plan.json` binds
every historical preparation input, the source export, seed and finite resource
settings. Historical training, development and test structures are all treated
as exposed; their labels are not used.

Eight actual generator constructions are scheduled: papers, overlap,
disjointness, conjunct removal, participant, domain, range and filler. Named
generator aliases are reported separately. A typed OWL incidence fingerprint
ignores entity names, mirrored side names, annotations and disconnected nuisance
components. It groups the largest editable connected core in both the observed
and intended theory; disconnected editable distractors do not create new
parents. Conservative fingerprint collisions reduce coverage. These fingerprints
establish structural grouping, not logical non-equivalence or eight previously
unseen mechanism families. Historical-family exposure remains explicit.

Before measuring outcomes, the stage selects two profiling parents per
construction, then provisionally assigns distinct structural groups to the
8/2/4 training/development/test design and four additional fresh-evaluation
parents. The remaining groups are hash-ordered before split allocation to avoid
assigning larger cases systematically to test. Every missing target is retained.
The new evaluation cohort is disjoint from the larger-training test cohort.
Only profiling parents are materialized and measured in this stage. Their clean
and corrupted variants remain paired; all other splits remain a provisional
structural schedule until the separately registered corpus-finalization stage.

Profiles measure observable retrieval and native semantic family compilation,
with stage-1 grammar depth/constructor bounds and fixed resource settings. Each
variant has cold and requested-warm probes in separate bounded worker processes.
Actual cache states and every template outcome are carried by native telemetry;
a warm request is not evidence that every family was a cache hit. The manifest
uses 180 seconds/12288 MiB per outer case probe, 60 seconds per object family
aggregate, 20 seconds per native call and 8192 MiB native RSS. All attempted,
partial, failed and unknown rows remain in the 64-row target denominator.

Cold attempts use new cache directories; resumed warm probes bind their cold
receipt. Checkpoints bind the manifest, generator/preparation code, repair code,
native implementations and dependency versions. Batch accounting reserves and
settles every attempt in the existing cumulative ledger. The initial six-hour
logical allowance is operational and may be continued compatibly with all costs
preserved. It is not a scientific budget or campaign time ceiling.

This stage does not train, query teachers, evaluate held-out cases, establish
G0–G2 or claim learning efficiency. The registry retains corpus finalization and
numerical development profiling as preparation stages. Corpus-dependent studies
wait for the finalization receipt; larger training additionally waits for the
numerical profile and the existing qualification/historical dependencies. Further
labeling, training and evaluation descendants must remain registered until their
actual receipts exist. No frozen pilot checkpoint or historical design changes.
