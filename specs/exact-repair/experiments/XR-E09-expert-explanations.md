# XR-E09 — Preferences and inspectable repairs

Research question: RQ4.

## Data

Generated profile simulations initially; actual complete-repair pairwise choices only if collected with a declared protocol.

## Comparison

Compare default cost weights, simulated profiles and a regularised nonnegative learned cost vector with a fixed semantic model. Evaluate profile shifts separately from factual or hard-policy changes.

## Measurements

Measure held-out preference prediction, repair changes by edit provenance, semantic benefit/cost tradeoffs and whether explanations identify exact changed occurrences and affected consequences.

## Interpretation and acceptance

No expert feedback is required to start. Simulations are not evidence of real user satisfaction. Higher ontology-edit costs and solver optimality are not calibrated confidence about the intended axiom.

Shared controls, split rules, resources and status reporting are defined in [02](../02-experimental-protocol.md).
