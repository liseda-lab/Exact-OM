# XR-E06 — Transfer to real ontology alignments

Research question: RQ2/RQ5.

## Data

Generated cases, Conference 2025, Bio-ML 2024 selected subsets and Bio-ML 2026 whole pairs as separate cohorts.

## Comparison

Compare generated-only transfer and training-side real adaptation; hold out structural families, complete pairs and where possible ontologies. Compare matcher sources as separate strata.

## Measurements

Report case/pair/ontology counts, semantic metrics supported by available labels, verification coverage, runtime and per-pair results; use public counts in [11](../11-benchmark-evidence.md) as context only.

## Interpretation and acceptance

All sibling outputs and corruptions stay together. References are partial correspondence labels, not complete repair preferences. Three whole biomedical pairs cannot support strong population claims from thousands of mapping rows.

Shared controls, split rules, resources and status reporting are defined in [02](../02-experimental-protocol.md).
