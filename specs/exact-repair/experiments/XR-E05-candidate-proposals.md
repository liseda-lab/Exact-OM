# XR-E05 — Constrained expression generation

Research question: RQ3.

## Data

Small enumerable grammars for conformance; generated and held-out real candidate menus.

## Comparison

Compare bounded enumeration, uniform constrained sampling, unconstrained generation plus rejection, conditioned product (one component), conditioned mixture and an optional PSDD alternative. Match menus, expression budgets and evaluation calls.

## Measurements

Measure exact grammar-valid fraction, useful-candidate recall, verified repair utility at fixed pool size, duplicate rate, normaliser failures and compilation/sampling time/memory.

## Interpretation and acceptance

Verify probabilities against enumeration and mixture posterior weights. Separate retrieval, grammar, compilation and sampling omissions. A grammar-valid candidate may be globally inconsistent.

Shared controls, split rules, resources and status reporting are defined in [02](../02-experimental-protocol.md).
