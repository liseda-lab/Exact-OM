# XR-E05 — Circuits with ontology-context constraints

**Target:** XR-2.1, 30 September 2026. **Question:** RQ3. Requirements for new runs; archived XR-2 results remain separate.

## Data

Tiny enumerable grammars, the pilot failure families, fresh generated vocabularies and training/held-out real menus with labelled proof scope.

## Comparisons

First repair native reference management and evaluate small family SDDs. Compare grammar-only circuits, ontology-informed circuits, and a decoder enforcing identical semantic conditions; include bounded enumeration, uniform and learned weights. Evaluate alternative vtrees, one/four mixture components and persistent cache cold/warm separately.

## Measurements

Enumerated support/probability/gradient agreement; grammar validity and exact contextual scope; useful verified coverage; duplicates; branch failures; live/dead/allocated/root/element counts and RSS; build/sample/verification/total time; amortisation versus number of calls.

## Acceptance and interpretation

Preserve fixed-bit and family-validity mass and sum duplicate encodings. Editable proofs cannot permanently ban candidates. Do not shrink menus silently or claim full-pool generation when a family failed. Compare semantic equivalence of constraints, not syntax-only decoder versus semantically informed circuit. A new inside/outside distribution is a named optional fallback, not an equivalent refactor without proof.
