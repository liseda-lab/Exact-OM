# XR-E02 — Repair actions and retained meaning

**Target:** XR-2.1, 30 September 2026. **Question:** RQ1, RQ4. Requirements for new runs; archived XR-2 results remain separate.

## Data

Generated examples for every action and mirror direction, mixed mapping/ontology defects, training-side real corruptions and held-out actual alignments.

## Comparisons

Nested language capacity: keep/delete; retained directions; endpoint substitution; subclass-expression specialisation; necessary conditions; complex equivalence; eligible ontology edits. Also remove each family from a common full inventory. Compare symbolic and learned selection with the same access to richer actions.

## Measurements

Independent desired/unwanted consequences, non-vacuity, weak semantic-fidelity comparisons, explicit cost/edit provenance, useful candidate coverage and exact regret only on a complete small universe.

## Acceptance and interpretation

A necessary condition is not generally a weakening and may retain a conflicting direction. Report complete emitted bundles and precise occurrence patches. Ontology-edit penalties are caution, not evidence of author intent. Separate fixed-pool expressiveness from actual generation cost and verify richer outputs under the same policy.
