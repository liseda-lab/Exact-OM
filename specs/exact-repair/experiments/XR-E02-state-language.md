# XR-E02 — Repair actions and expressiveness

Research question: RQ1/RQ4.

## Data

Generated action fixtures, mixed corruptions, real-structure corruptions and held-out Conference/Bio-ML outputs.

## Comparison

Nested candidate languages: delete/keep; directional subsumption; endpoint substitution; subclass specialisation and necessary conditions; complex equivalence; eligible ontology edits. Also remove each action family from the full model.

## Measurements

Measure verified feasibility, independent semantic benefit, exact small-case regret, retained consequences, edits by provenance, expression complexity and missing-candidate causes.

## Interpretation and acceptance

Use matched teacher queries and verification scopes. Compare both matched-pool action availability and full-system cost. Logical safety alone cannot establish a richer repair is better.

Shared controls, split rules, resources and status reporting are defined in [02](../02-experimental-protocol.md).
